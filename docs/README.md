# SecretDoor documentation

SecretDoor is an offline password manager for Android. These pages explain how to use it and what
it does with your data. The links behind the **?** buttons and **Learn more** links inside the app
open the pages below.

## Start here

- [Privacy](PRIVACY.md): what the app stores, what leaves your phone (almost nothing) and which
  permissions it asks for.
- [Backups](help/backups.md): how to protect yourself against losing your phone.
- [Managing vaults](help/managing-vaults.md): rename, delete and add vaults.
- [Security](help/security.md): how your vault is encrypted and what SecretDoor can and cannot
  protect you from.

## Using SecretDoor

- [Autofill](help/autofill.md): fill logins and cards in apps and browsers.
- [Generator](help/generator.md): passwords, passphrases and usernames.
- [Authenticator codes](help/authenticator-codes.md): store and use two-step verification codes.
- [SSH keys](help/ssh-keys.md): generate or import SSH keys and keep them in your vault.
- [URI match detection](help/uri-match-detection.md): which saved logins show up for a website
  or app.
- [Master password re-prompt](help/master-password-reprompt.md): ask for your master password
  again before sensitive items are used.
- [Importing data](help/importing-data.md): bring in items from a backup or another app.
- [Flight recorder](help/flight-recorder.md): diagnostic logs for bug reports.
- [Premium](help/premium.md): what "Premium" means in SecretDoor.
