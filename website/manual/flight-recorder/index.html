Flight recorder

The flight recorder writes diagnostic information about what the app is doing to a log file on your phone. If SecretDoor misbehaves, a recorded log helps a developer find out why.

What it records

  • The app's log messages: errors, warnings and notes about what the app is doing.
  • Basic device and app version information.

The recorder is meant to avoid secrets such as your master password, vault items and encryption keys, but it is a diagnostic log, so read it before you share it (see below).

How to use it

  1. Open Settings > About and switch on Flight recorder.
  2. Choose how long it should record: 1 hour, 8 hours, 24 hours or 1 week. It stops by itself.
  3. Reproduce the problem.
  4. Open View recorded logs to share or delete a log.

While it is on, a banner reminds you and shows when it will stop.

Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. The log stays on your phone until you share it yourself, for example by attaching it to a bug report. Delete recorded logs from the same screen when you no longer need them.
