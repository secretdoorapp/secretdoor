Master password re-prompt

Master password re-prompt is an option you can turn on for a single item. When it is on, SecretDoor asks for your master password again before it will:

  • show or copy the item's secrets (password, card number, security code, private key, hidden fields),
  • fill the item into an app or website with autofill.

Why use it

Your vault is already unlocked while you use the app. If someone borrows your unlocked phone, or the app is left open, they could read everything in it. Re-prompt adds a second check for the items that matter most, such as banking logins or recovery codes.

It does not make the item's encryption any stronger. It is a safeguard against someone using your already-unlocked vault, not against someone who has the vault file and your master password.

How to turn it on

  1. Open the item and tap Edit.
  2. Switch on Master password re-prompt.
  3. Save.

Turn it off the same way.
