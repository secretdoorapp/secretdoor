Built different

A vault, not a service.

Most password managers keep a copy of your vault on their servers. SecretDoor has no servers, so there is nothing to breach, sell or switch off.

Local only

Your vault lives on your phone and nowhere else. There is nothing to sign in to and no company that holds a copy of your data.

Strongly encrypted

A key only your master password can unlock, using Argon2id and AES-256 through the audited Bitwarden SDK.

Backups you control

Save an encrypted backup file anywhere, or schedule daily or weekly backups to a file or cloud folder you choose, such as Google Drive.

More than passwords

Logins, cards, identities, secure notes, SSH keys, bank accounts, driver’s licenses, passports and passkeys, with built-in authenticator codes.

Autofill and biometrics

Fill logins in apps and browsers, and unlock with your fingerprint or face.

Free and open source

Every feature is free. The code is public under the GNU GPL v3, so anyone can check what the app does.

What leaves your phone?

Almost nothing. SecretDoor has no analytics and no crash reporting, and it never downloads website icons, because that would tell a server which sites you use. The only optional network use is a breach check for a new master password. It is off by default and sends just the first five characters of a hash, never the password.

One thing to know.

Because there is no server, nobody can recover your vault or reset your master password, not even us. Make a backup and keep it somewhere other than your phone.
